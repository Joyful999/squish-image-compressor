About us

Image compression that respects your privacy

Squish started from a simple frustration: every online image compressor meant uploading personal photos to a stranger's server. We built the alternative.

Why we built Squish

Most "free" image compressors quietly send your files to a remote server, compress them there, and hand back a download link. That works, but it means your photos, documents, and screenshots pass through infrastructure you don't control — and sometimes get stored longer than you'd expect.

Squish takes a different approach. Every resize and compression pass happens using your browser's own Canvas API, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded. There's no server in the loop at all, which also makes Squish fast — there's no round trip to wait for.

What makes Squish different

  • No uploads, ever. Your images are read directly into browser memory and never leave your device.
  • No account required. Open the page and start compressing immediately.
  • Batch friendly. Compress dozens of images in one session and download them individually or as a ZIP.
  • Full control. Adjust quality, resize dimensions, and convert formats before you export.

Who Squish is for

Bloggers preparing images for faster page loads, online sellers optimizing product photos, students shrinking scans for email attachments, and anyone who just needs a smaller file without installing software or trusting a third party with their pictures.
