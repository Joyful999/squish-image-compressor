Frequently asked questions

Is Squish really free to use?

Yes. Squish is completely free with no sign-up, no watermarks, and no hidden limits on the number of images you compress.

Do my images get uploaded to a server?

No. All compression happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

Which image formats are supported?

Squish supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP files up to 50MB each, and can convert between these formats on export.

Will compression reduce image quality?

You control the quality level with a slider from 10 to 100 percent, so you can balance file size against visual quality for your own use case.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Yes. Upload as many images as you like, compress them together, and download them individually or as a single ZIP file.
