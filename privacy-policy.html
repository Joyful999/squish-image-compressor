Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. The short version: your images never leave your device.

1. Your images stay on your device

Squish compresses images entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. When you select or drop a file, it's loaded into your browser's memory and processed locally. At no point is the image data transmitted to Squish's servers or any third party — because no such upload ever occurs.

2. What we do collect

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic, aggregated technical information such as approximate location, browser type, and page visited, for security and analytics purposes. This is standard web server behavior and is unrelated to the images you compress.

3. Cookies and local storage

Squish uses your browser's local storage only to remember your light/dark theme preference. We do not use tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share data with advertisers.

4. Third-party resources

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and the JSZip library from a public CDN so that batch downloads work. These providers may log standard request data (such as IP address) as part of serving those files, in line with their own privacy policies.

5. Advertisements

Squish may display third-party advertisements. Ad networks can use cookies to personalize the ads you see. You can control ad personalization through your browser settings or your ad network's opt-out tools.

6. Children's privacy

Squish is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of Squish after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our contact page.
